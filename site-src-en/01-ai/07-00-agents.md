## Agents {#sec:agents}

What separates a model that can only answer questions from an assistant that can finish the job for you? Not, mostly, being "smarter" — mostly **hands and feet**: it can call tools, remember context, and break one job into several steps and carry them out. This chapter is about the engineering that turns a "model" into an "agent" — fitting the hands and feet first (the harness), then how to make it better and better at the work (RSI and multi-agent), and finally how it borrows tools and knowledge from outside (MCP, skills, retrieval).
