## Machine learning {#sec:machine-learning}

This chapter scales "learning" up from a single neuron to a whole discipline: first where **depth** comes from (how stacking layers taught a network to bend), then the three learning paradigms — learning against the answer key, finding structure on your own, and trial and error against the world; and finally the most practical question of all: **how do you tell whether it has actually learned anything?**

The three sections stand fairly independent; [Section 1.4.3](#sec:generalization) also works as a standalone read — it skips the theory and talks only about how to sign off.
